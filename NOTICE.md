## Classical root dictionaries

Edward William Lane, **Arabic-English Lexicon** (19th century): historical dictionary text is in the public domain. Digital entry text, IDs and page references use the GPL-licensed Lane's Lexicon database, <https://github.com/laneslexicon/LexiconDatabase/tree/b371ab16b963def3085674a516e5f45d9c787b1d>. The source archive is pinned by URL and SHA-256 in `scripts/dictionary-sources-lock.json`.

Ibn Faris (d. 1004), **Maqayis al-Lugha**, and Ibn Manzur (d. 1311), **Lisan al-Arab**: historical Arabic dictionary text is in the public domain. Digital entries and IDs use the classical tables supplied by <https://github.com/wizsk/arabic_lexicons/tree/a34dc0aaa4e45ff245a9bc7213747838c6e51b5a>. The archive also contains other dictionaries; Qexplore does not export those tables. The source archive is pinned by URL and SHA-256 in `scripts/dictionary-sources-lock.json`.

Qexplore's separate Urdu root summaries are concise, AI-assisted editorial renderings of selected general lexical senses, not a published Urdu edition or a specialist-reviewed translation. They are distributed with the application under GPL v3.0 or later. Each record retains original excerpts, dictionary entry identifiers and, for Lane, page references. The two unresolved root analyses are marked instead of being assigned a definition. Source attribution implies no affiliation.

## Translation scope

Verse-word meanings are preserved source glosses. Root-family entries show source meanings of related words, with citations and review status. General root meanings are separate editorial Urdu summaries of classical dictionary entries, with their AI assistance disclosed. Neither dataset is exhaustive or a claim that every root has one fixed meaning. Historical excerpts retain their original wording; they may include historical or religious vocabulary. No doctrinal conclusions, tafsir dataset, or generated full-verse interpretation is added.
