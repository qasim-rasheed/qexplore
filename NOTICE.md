## Greentech Apps Foundation word-by-word corpus

Arabic written words and Urdu word glosses: Greentech Apps Foundation, <https://quran.gtaf.org>, public word-by-word data, via the reference files in QuranicWords. Original Arabic and Urdu tokens are preserved verbatim. Source-specific content terms continue to apply; attribution is not a claim of affiliation or ownership.

## QuranicWords

Urdu lemma glosses, review flags, and citations: QuranicWords open vocabulary dataset v1.0.0, DEANY BYTES, GNU GPL v3.0 or later. Snapshot: <https://github.com/rmrashahriar/QuranicWords/tree/635f923a990c41add1df0d57e8bf96b2d18c6077>. This project uses vocabulary, source word glosses, and surah names, not upstream full-verse translations, lesson commentary, application UI, or religious branding. Meaning review flags remain visible. Morphologically inconsistent lexical citations are excluded by validation.

## Translation scope

Word meanings are source glosses, not generated claims of a context-free or ideology-free dictionary. Root-family entries show source meanings of related words, with citations and review status. They are not exhaustive root definitions. No AI-generated translation, doctrinal conclusions, tafsir, or full-verse interpretation is bundled.
